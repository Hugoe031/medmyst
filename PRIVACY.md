# Privacy Policy for MedMyst

**Last Updated: 2026-11-14**

MedMyst ("we", "us", or "our") provides a clinical case arcade application for educational purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
MedMyst is designed as a **local-first** application. This means:
- **Personal Data**: We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share your name.
- **Local Storage**: Your game progress (XP, streaks, badges), clinical statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device using `localStorage`. We do not have access to this data on our servers.
- **No Cloud Sync**: Since data is stored locally, it will be lost if you uninstall the app or clear your browser/app data.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
To keep the app free, we show rewarded advertisements (e.g., watching a video to earn a hint).
- **Service Provider**: We use Google AdMob to serve ads.
- **Data Collection**: Google may collect and use certain data (like your device's advertising ID) to serve personalized ads.
- **Your Choices**: You can manage your ad preferences through the "Privacy Settings" button in the app's Settings menu. We support GDPR/UMP consent forms for users in regulated regions.

## 3. iOS App Tracking (ATT)
If you are using the iOS version of MedMyst, we will ask for your permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can decline this request at any time in your iOS settings.

## 4. Third-Party Links
The app contains links to external medical sources (e.g., CDC, ACOG, PubMed). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party websites.

## 5. Children's Privacy
MedMyst is intended for nursing and medical students and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official store listing.
