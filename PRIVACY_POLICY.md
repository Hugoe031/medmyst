# Privacy Policy for MedMyst

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026 — last updated for the v1.0 Android release.

MedMyst ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the MedMyst mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
MedMyst is designed with a privacy-first approach. 

*   **Personal Data:** We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not require you to create an account.
*   **Local Storage:** All game progress, including XP, streaks, and category mastery, is stored **locally on your device**. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
*   **Device Information:** We may collect non-identifying information about the type of device you use and its operating system version to ensure app compatibility.
*   **Notifications:** If you opt in, MedMyst schedules one local reminder a day at a time you choose. The reminder is created and stored on your device; it is never sent from a server. You can turn it off at any time in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

## 2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)
We use **Google AdMob** to display rewarded advertisements, which allow you to earn in-game hints. 
*   AdMob may use advertising identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized ads.
*   You can manage your ad personalization preferences through your device's Google Settings.
*   For more information, please visit [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)
If you use the iOS build, MedMyst asks for permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can decline, and you can change the decision at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## 4. Children's Privacy
MedMyst is intended for educational use by medical students and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 4. Data Security
Since your data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. If you delete the app, your local data will be permanently removed.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to us via our app store listing contact information.
